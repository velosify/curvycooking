Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Curvy Cooking ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account info: name, email address, and a hashed password when you create an account.
  • Payment info: payment details are collected and processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number. We receive an order ID, the amount, your email, and your billing zip code.
  • Usage data: basic analytics (pages visited, device type, country) via Cloudflare and Meta Pixel.
  • Communications: emails you send to us and our responses.

How we use it

  • To deliver your Curvy Cookbook purchase and give you access to the members area.
  • To send you account-related emails (signup link, password resets, receipts).
  • To improve the site and measure marketing performance.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your email with third parties for marketing.

Third parties we use

  • Stripe: payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare: hosting, DNS, analytics. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Railway: backend hosting and database. Railway Privacy Policy
  • Resend: transactional email delivery. Resend Privacy Policy
  • Meta: Meta Pixel for ad measurement. Meta Privacy Policy

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account entirely. Email hello@curvycooking.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account with us. If you delete your account, we remove your personal info within 30 days, except for transaction records we're legally required to keep (typically 7 years for tax purposes).

Contact
