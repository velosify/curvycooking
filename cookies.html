Cookies Policy

Last updated:

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies we use on curvycooking.com. Not the bake-able kind, sorry.

What's a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your browser. Sites use cookies to remember things like whether you're logged in, what's in your cart, or which articles you've already read.

What we use

Strictly necessary

These keep the site working. You can't opt out without breaking essential functionality.

  • cc_session: keeps you signed in to the members area. Set by our server (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=None). Expires after 30 days.
  • Stripe cookies: used by Stripe to process your payment securely and detect fraud. Required to complete checkout.

Analytics & advertising

These help us understand how the site is used and measure ad performance. We use these unless you've opted out via your browser's "Do Not Track" or our cookie banner.

  • Meta Pixel (_fbp, _fbc): measures ad performance and shows you Curvy Cooking ads on Facebook and Instagram when relevant. Set by Meta. Lifetime: up to 90 days.
  • Cloudflare Analytics: privacy-friendly site analytics. No personally identifiable info collected.

How to control cookies

You can clear cookies anytime through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

You can also block all third-party cookies in your browser settings. This will disable our Meta Pixel without affecting your ability to log in or complete a purchase.

Do Not Track

Our site respects the "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser signal. When DNT is enabled, we don't load the Meta Pixel.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
